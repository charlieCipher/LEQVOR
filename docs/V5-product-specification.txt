Aureva V5 — Confirmed Product & Security Architecture
1. Final product definition

Aureva is a private family continuity platform that protects and connects important assets, records, trusted people, instructions and access rules, so the right information remains understandable and accessible when circumstances change.

Aureva is not primarily:

cloud storage,
a password manager,
a will replacement,
an AI adviser,
an investment manager,
an inheritance-transfer service.

The central idea remains the one already present in your original design: the real problem is continuity of information, because documents, assets, contacts and instructions are usually scattered across multiple places.

Aureva's five questions

Every important record should answer:

WHAT exists?

WHY does it matter?

WHERE is it / where is the original?

WHO needs to know?

WHAT should happen next?

That is the actual product.

2. Confirmed core principles

Your original architecture already established zero-knowledge, recovery-first, family continuity and simplicity. Those stay.

We now freeze six principles:

Principle	Confirmed rule
Zero Knowledge	Aureva servers must not require plaintext vault contents
Recovery First	A secure vault that cannot be recovered is a failed vault
Continuity First	Information must remain understandable, not merely stored
Least Privilege	Every person/device/integration gets only required access
Independence	Users must eventually be able to recover/export without relying forever on Aureva
Simplicity	Complexity is allowed only when it materially improves protection, recovery or continuity

And one additional permanent product rule:

Aureva will not use AI to analyse private vault contents.

No AI assistant. No AI summaries. No “chat with your legacy.” The original V4 architecture already deferred AI because of the zero-knowledge conflict.

We are making that decision permanent.

28. Recovery architecture

Recovery remains one of Aureva's highest priorities. Your original architecture correctly describes recovery failure as catastrophic.

Use a client-generated recovery secret.

Conceptually:

24-word Recovery Secret
          ↓
Recovery Key
          ↓
Recovery KEK
          ↓
Decrypt recovery-wrapped VMK
          ↓
Vault recovered

The recovery phrase must never be stored by Aureva in plaintext.

Your existing design already says the recovery phrase is generated and verified client-side.

77. Final Aureva North Star

Every product decision now gets tested against this:

If the owner becomes unavailable tomorrow, can the correct trusted person understand what exists, why it matters, where it is, and what should happen next, while Aureva itself learns as little private information as reasonably possible?

Then ask only three engineering questions:

Does this improve SECURITY?

Does this improve RECOVERY?

Does this improve CONTINUITY?

If all three answers are no, the feature waits.
